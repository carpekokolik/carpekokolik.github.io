Pairwise – Privacy Policy Effective 11 October 2026 Pairwise is a puzzle game for iOS and Android, published by Olga Kaplenkova (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is processed when you play Pairwise, who processes it, and what choices you have. Contact: carpekokolik@gmail.com In short Pairwise has no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress is stored only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google processes device data to deliver and measure those ads, and to personalise them if you allow it. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. Information stored on your device The game saves your progress, your settings, your HP balance and a record of which purchases have already been delivered. This is kept in the app’s own storage on your device and is not sent to us. It may be included in your device’s own backups (iCloud or Google) if you have those turned on. It is removed when you delete the app or clear its data. Advertising Pairwise shows two kinds of ads: a video you can choose to watch for a reward, and a full-screen ad between puzzles. Both are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited for players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and of Google LLC elsewhere. To show ads, Google’s software inside the game collects and processes: device identifiers, including the advertising ID on Android and, on iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking; your IP address, from which an approximate location such as country or city is derived; device and app details such as model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version; which ads were shown and how you interacted with them; diagnostic and performance data. Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform, and detect fraud and abuse. With your permission it is also used to show ads based on your interests. Google may work with other advertising companies for these purposes; where a consent form is shown, it lists them. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. We see only summary reports, such as how many ads were shown. How Google uses information from apps that use its services Google Privacy Policy Your choices about ads European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Before any ad is requested, the game asks for your consent. You can agree, decline, or choose purpose by purpose and partner by partner. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are not based on your interests. You can change your answer at any time: open Settings in the game and tap Privacy. iOS. iOS may ask whether Pairwise is allowed to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites. You can change this in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android Settings app, in the Ads section (under Privacy or Google, depending on your device). Purchases You can buy extra HP in the game. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing details. The game receives a confirmation from the store that the purchase was completed, and keeps a record of it on your device so that the item is delivered once. Apple Privacy Policy Google Privacy Policy Legal bases Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, the processing described above relies on: your consent, for storing or reading information on your device for advertising and for personalised ads. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Privacy in the game; legitimate interests, for delivering and measuring non-personalised ads that fund the game, and for protecting against fraud and abuse; performance of a contract, for providing the game and delivering items you buy. Children Pairwise is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us. How long information is kept We keep no personal information on servers of our own. Information stored on your device stays there until you delete the app or clear its data. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies. Transfers to other countries Google may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google describes the safeguards it relies on for such transfers in its data transfer frameworks. Your rights Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live. Because we hold no information that identifies you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google, for example through My Ad Center or the advertising settings on your device. You can also write to us at the address below and we will help where we can. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
